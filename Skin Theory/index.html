Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

We do not run a checkout. Send us the product name and quantity through the enquiry form and we reply with payment and delivery details within one business day.

What does 100% natural actually mean here?

Every formulation is built from plant oils, butters and botanical extracts. We use no mineral oil, no parabens and no synthetic dyes. Scented products are perfumed with essential oils only.

Which products suit eczema-prone or sensitive skin?

Choose from the fragrance-free range: Dermacare Sensitive Oil, Cloud Skin, Barrier Repair Oil and Cloud Skin Lotion. Patch test on a small area first, as you would with any new product.

Are the products safe to use during pregnancy?

Mother and Me and Elastic Glow are made for pregnancy and postpartum skin. Glow Lo contains retinol, so we do recommend it while you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

How long do the products last?

Twelve months unopened, and six months once opened. Because there are no synthetic preservatives, keep bottles closed, dry and out of direct sunlight.

Do you ship outside South Africa?

Yes. We ship from our London and Cape Town studios. Shipping is free on South African orders over R800, and international rates are quoted per order.

Can I return a product?

Unopened products can be returned within 14 days of delivery. If an order arrives damaged, email us a photo and we replace it at no cost.
