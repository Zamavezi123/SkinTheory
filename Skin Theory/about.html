About us

Our Story

Skin Theory was founded in 1987 in London, born from a belief that true luxury lies in restraint rather than excess. In a market saturated with sprawling skincare ranges, we chose the opposite path. We have committed to two categories to ensure we perfect them rather than expanding beyond them. Therefore, we are sure that with at least one of our products, your skin will feel rejuvenated, nourished, and noticeably radiant. Every formulation is developed carefully with natural ingredients, as we are a brand that excels in quality to ensure you have bright, soft, and smooth skin.

WE ARE THE GLOW FORMULA

Mission

To craft body care essentials of uncompromising quality, while celebrating every skin tone, type, and story. We ensure you feel confident in your skin without compromise because healthy, luminous skin is the foundation of feeling good.

Vision

Our vision as Skin Theory is to be regarded as a benchmark British name in natural body care, defined by refinement rather than sheer product volume.

Our Philosophy

Skin thrives when you keep it simple, especially with Skin Theory providing strong results with a gentle approach.

We provide no toxins, no drama, just letting your skin breathe. You don't have to strip, peel, burn, or harm your skin to see your results.
